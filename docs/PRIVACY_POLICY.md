# ATHLETEN PRIVACY POLICY

**Effective date:** 17 September 2026  
**Provider / Data Controller:** Nova Code  
**Product:** AthleteN

> This Privacy Policy is a product-specific draft based on the current AthleteN architecture described in the repository. It must be reconciled with actual production data flows, vendors, retention periods, security controls, consent/notice flows, and the final legal identity and contact details before launch.

## 1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how personal information may be processed when a person uses AthleteN, including its web application, mobile application when released, athlete and academy features, verification workflows, support functions, subscriptions, and AI-assisted features.

## 2. Information We May Process

Depending on the features used, AthleteN may process:

- account and authentication information, such as email address and account identifiers;
- profile information, such as name, date of birth, gender, sport, club or academy, coach, and related athlete information;
- training, attendance, tournament, match, medal, goal, checklist, and performance records;
- verification information and documents submitted for student, athlete, academy, or other verification;
- competition and weight-related records entered by a user;
- support requests, feedback, announcements, and communications;
- subscription and entitlement information;
- technical information required for security, authentication, diagnostics, and service operation;
- AI prompts, inputs, outputs, and usage information to the extent required to provide or secure an AI feature; and
- information contained in files or other content that a user voluntarily submits.

AthleteN should collect only information reasonably necessary for the stated feature or service.

## 3. Sensitive and Children's Data

Some athlete information may be sensitive or otherwise subject to additional legal protection depending on the applicable law and context. AthleteN should apply appropriate access controls and handling procedures.

AthleteN may be used by children or teenagers. Where children's personal data is processed, AthleteN will apply the safeguards, consent requirements, parental or guardian mechanisms, restrictions, and other obligations required by applicable law at the time of processing.

The service must not treat a minor's acceptance of terms as a substitute for legally required parental or guardian consent.

## 4. Sources of Information

Information may be provided directly by the user, by a parent or guardian where appropriate, by an academy or coach where the feature permits it and the relevant authority exists, or generated through the user's interaction with AthleteN.

Information may also be received from service providers that support authentication, hosting, storage, payments, communications, security, or other functions, subject to applicable law and contractual arrangements.

## 5. Purposes of Processing

Personal information may be processed to:

- create and secure accounts;
- provide AthleteN features requested by the user;
- maintain athlete, training, competition, academy, and related records;
- perform verification and prevent fraud or abuse;
- provide support and respond to requests;
- administer subscriptions and payments;
- provide, secure, debug, and improve services;
- operate AI-assisted features when enabled;
- maintain audit, security, and operational records;
- comply with legal obligations; and
- protect users, the service, and legal rights.

AthleteN should not use personal information for a new materially incompatible purpose without an appropriate lawful basis, notice, consent, or other process required by applicable law.

## 6. Lawful Basis

The applicable lawful basis for processing depends on the feature, user, jurisdiction, and law in force at the time. Depending on the circumstances, processing may rely on consent, performance of a requested service, legal obligation, legitimate use or another lawful basis recognised by applicable law.

Where consent is required, it should be obtained through an appropriate notice and consent mechanism and should not be represented as having been obtained merely because a person viewed this document.

## 7. AI Features

If an AI feature is enabled, information supplied to that feature may be processed to generate the requested output and to maintain security, usage limits, reliability, and service operation.

AthleteN should minimise personal information sent to AI providers and should use appropriate contractual, technical, and organisational safeguards.

AI output is not itself a statement of fact and should not be treated as medical, legal, financial, or professional advice.

## 8. Sharing and Service Providers

AthleteN may use service providers for infrastructure and operations, such as authentication, database and storage hosting, application hosting, payments, email, security, analytics, and AI processing.

A provider should receive only the information reasonably necessary for its function and should be bound by appropriate contractual or legal safeguards where required.

AthleteN does not grant third parties permission to use personal information for their own unrelated purposes merely because they provide infrastructure to AthleteN.

## 9. International Transfers

Some service providers may process information outside India. Where personal information is transferred across borders, the transfer will be subject to applicable law and any restrictions, contractual safeguards, notices, or other measures required at the relevant time.

## 10. Retention

Personal information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the stated purpose, legal obligations, dispute resolution, security, fraud prevention, or other lawful requirements.

Actual retention periods should be documented internally by data category and feature before commercial launch.

## 11. Security

AthleteN uses or is designed to use technical and organisational safeguards including authenticated access, row-level access controls, private storage where appropriate, server-side handling of secrets, and restricted administrative access.

No security system can guarantee absolute security. Users should protect credentials and promptly report suspected unauthorised access.

## 12. User and Data Principal Rights

Depending on the applicable law and the user's status, a person may have rights concerning access to information, correction, erasure, grievance redressal, consent withdrawal, nomination, restriction or objection, portability, or other rights.

Requests will be handled according to the law applicable to the request and the relevant data controller or fiduciary role.

## 13. Account Deletion

Where AthleteN provides account deletion or erasure functionality, the request will be processed subject to applicable law, legitimate retention requirements, security records, financial records, dispute preservation, and other lawful exceptions.

Deleting an account does not necessarily erase information that must lawfully be retained.

## 14. Cookies and Similar Technologies

AthleteN may use cookies, local storage, session mechanisms, or similar technologies required for authentication, security, preferences, and service operation. Optional analytics or other technologies should be disclosed and handled according to applicable law and the relevant consent requirements.

## 15. Payments

Payment details may be processed directly by third-party payment providers rather than stored by AthleteN, depending on the implementation. Payment providers may process information under their own privacy notices and terms.

## 16. Children's Privacy

AthleteN is designed with the possibility of under-18 users in mind. Product flows must implement any age-gating, parental/guardian consent, advertising restrictions, behavioural monitoring restrictions, or other child-specific safeguards required by applicable law before the relevant feature is made available to children.

## 17. Changes

This Policy may be updated when services, processing activities, law, or security practices change. Where law requires notice or consent for a material change, AthleteN will follow the required process.
