# ATHLETEN CONTRIBUTOR AGREEMENT

**Effective date:** 17 September 2026  
**Project:** AthleteN  
**Rights holder:** Nova Code

> This agreement is a template for voluntary contributors. It should be reviewed by qualified counsel and, where appropriate, replaced or supplemented by employment or contractor agreements.

## 1. Purpose

This Contributor Agreement governs voluntary submissions made to the AthleteN project when the contributor and Nova Code have agreed that this document applies.

It does not automatically apply to employees, contractors, agencies, vendors, or other persons whose intellectual-property rights are governed by a separate written agreement.

## 2. Contribution

A “Contribution” means code, documentation, designs, graphics, tests, bug fixes, configuration, or other material intentionally submitted to AthleteN for possible inclusion in the project.

Submitting a Contribution does not guarantee acceptance or inclusion.

## 3. Contributor Rights and Authority

The contributor represents, to the extent legally applicable, that:

1. they have the authority to submit the Contribution;
2. the Contribution is not knowingly copied from material that cannot lawfully be contributed;
3. they have disclosed any known third-party licence or restriction affecting the Contribution; and
4. submission does not knowingly violate another person's intellectual-property, confidentiality, privacy, or contractual rights.

The contributor must not submit passwords, API keys, private keys, personal data, confidential third-party information, or other secrets unless expressly requested and authorised through a secure process.

## 4. Ownership and Licence

Unless a separate written agreement expressly provides otherwise, the contributor retains ownership of rights they lawfully own in an unaccepted Contribution.

For a Contribution that Nova Code accepts and incorporates, the contributor grants Nova Code a worldwide, non-exclusive, perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free licence, to the extent the contributor has the necessary rights, to reproduce, modify, adapt, publish, distribute, display, perform, sublicense, and otherwise use the Contribution as part of AthleteN and related services.

If the parties intend an assignment of ownership rather than a licence, they should execute a separate written assignment satisfying applicable law. This document should not be interpreted as an assignment where applicable law requires a formal assignment instrument and that requirement has not been satisfied.

## 5. Moral Rights and Similar Rights

To the extent permitted by applicable law, the contributor agrees not to assert rights in a manner that prevents Nova Code from exercising the licence granted above. Any waiver or consent concerning moral rights applies only to the extent such waiver or consent is legally valid.

## 6. Third-Party Material

A contributor must identify third-party material included in a Contribution and provide the applicable licence information when known.

A contributor must not intentionally submit material under incompatible terms without prior approval.

## 7. Privacy and Confidentiality

Contributors must not disclose or incorporate personal data, credentials, confidential information, security secrets, or private project information except as authorised and necessary for the Contribution.

Any personal data received during project work must be handled only for the authorised purpose and in accordance with applicable law and project instructions.

## 8. Security

Contributors must use authorised development environments and access methods. They must not attempt to access accounts, databases, infrastructure, credentials, or restricted resources beyond the permissions provided to them.

Security vulnerabilities should be reported through the project's designated security reporting channel rather than publicly disclosed before Nova Code has had a reasonable opportunity to assess them.

## 9. Review and Acceptance

Nova Code may review, reject, modify, remove, or request changes to any Contribution. Receipt or review does not constitute acceptance.

Nova Code may preserve project history and records necessary for security, compliance, attribution, development, and dispute resolution.

## 10. No Employment or Partnership

This Agreement does not create employment, partnership, agency, joint venture, franchise, or other relationship unless a separate written agreement creates such a relationship.

## 11. No Compensation Unless Agreed

Unless separately agreed in writing, voluntary contributions are made without a promise of payment, equity, employment, royalties, or other compensation.

## 12. Indemnity

To the extent permitted by applicable law, a contributor who knowingly breaches the representations in this Agreement may be responsible for losses directly resulting from that breach. This clause does not create an indemnity to the extent prohibited by applicable law.

## 13. Termination

Nova Code may stop accepting future Contributions from a contributor. The licence granted for an accepted Contribution is intended to survive termination unless a separate written agreement provides otherwise.

## 14. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by Indian law to the extent applicable, subject to mandatory laws applicable to the contributor or transaction.

## 15. Electronic Acceptance

Where legally valid, acceptance may occur through an electronic process that clearly identifies the contributor, the version of this Agreement, and the contributor's agreement to its terms.

## 16. Capacity and Authority

A person must not accept this Agreement on behalf of another person or organisation without authority. Where a contributor is a minor or otherwise lacks legal capacity, a legally appropriate parent, guardian, representative, or separate contractual process may be required.

## 17. Entire Agreement

This Agreement governs Contributions only to the extent it applies. A separate employment, contractor, assignment, confidentiality, or other written agreement controls where it expressly covers the same subject matter.
