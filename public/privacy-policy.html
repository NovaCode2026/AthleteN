AthleteN Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

AthleteN ("AthleteN", "we", "us", or "our") provides an athlete-focused platform for training, athlete profiles, coaching, tournament information, messaging, and related features. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, when it may be shared, and the choices available to users.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, AthleteN may collect:

  • Account information: name, email address, username, password-related authentication information, and account role.
  • Athlete and profile information: date of birth, sport, discipline, club or academy, coach information, training information, tournament information, and other profile details you choose to provide.
  • Training and performance information: training sessions, readiness or performance information, goals, notes, and related athlete-entered information.
  • Messages and reports: messages and information submitted when users report content or accounts.
  • Connected-service information: information made available through third-party services that you explicitly connect to AthleteN, such as supported Instagram features. The information available depends on the permissions granted and the service's own policies.
  • Transaction information: subscription or payment status and related transaction records. Payment card details are handled by the applicable payment processor rather than stored directly by AthleteN unless explicitly stated otherwise.
  • Technical information: device, browser, IP address, log, diagnostic, and security information needed to operate and protect the service.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • create and operate AthleteN accounts and features;
  • provide athlete, coach, training, tournament, messaging, and account functionality;
  • authenticate users and protect accounts and the service;
  • process subscriptions and maintain billing records;
  • provide features that users explicitly request, including supported third-party integrations;
  • respond to support requests and investigate reports;
  • detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, security incidents, and violations of our terms; and
  • improve reliability, performance, and the user experience.

3. Third-Party Services and Integrations

AthleteN may use service providers for hosting, authentication, databases, payments, analytics or diagnostics, email, and other infrastructure. We may also provide integrations with third-party platforms when a user chooses to connect an account.

When you connect a third-party account, that provider may separately process information under its own privacy policy and terms. You can disconnect supported integrations through the controls provided by AthleteN where available.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information as a standalone data product. We may share information with service providers that process it on our behalf, with connected third-party services when you request an integration, when necessary to provide features you use, or when required by law or to protect the rights, safety, and security of users and the service.

5. Public and Shared Information

Some information may be visible to other users when you choose to place it in a profile, send a message, join a coach or academy workflow, or otherwise use a feature designed for sharing. Do not submit information that you do not want other authorized users to see.

6. Children and Teen Users

AthleteN is designed with account-safety controls for younger users. Where required, we may use age verification and parent or guardian approval workflows. We do not knowingly request more personal information from a child than is reasonably necessary to provide the service.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided information in a way that requires action, contact us at novacode.create@gmail.com.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain account and transaction records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, meet legal obligations, and protect the service. Retention periods may differ depending on the type of information and the purpose for which it is used.

9. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, export, restrict, or delete certain personal information, or to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. You may also disconnect supported third-party integrations. To make a privacy request, contact novacode.create@gmail.com. We may need to verify the request before acting on it.

10. Cookies and Similar Technologies

AthleteN and its service providers may use cookies, local storage, or similar technologies that are necessary for authentication, security, preferences, and service functionality. Where additional technologies require consent under applicable law, we will provide the appropriate controls.

11. International Processing

AthleteN and its service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for cross-border processing.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, practices, or legal requirements change. The "Last updated" date above indicates when the policy was most recently revised. Material changes may be communicated through the service where appropriate.
