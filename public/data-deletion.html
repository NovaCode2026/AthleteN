AthleteN Account and Data Deletion

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Contact: novacode.create@gmail.com

How to request deletion

  1. Email from the account email address, if you can access it, and use the subject “AthleteN Account and Data Deletion Request”.
  2. Tell us the account email/username and whether you want account closure, deletion of personal data, or both. Do not email your password, government ID number, payment-card details or unnecessary sensitive information.
  3. We may ask for proportionate verification to protect the account holder. If the request concerns a child, a parent/guardian or other lawful representative may need to demonstrate authority.
  4. We will acknowledge and process the request according to applicable law and the verified identity/authority. If we cannot delete particular records, we will explain the relevant legal or operational reason where required.

What deletion means

Account deletion may remove access to the account and associated athlete records. Certain records may need to be retained for legal, tax/accounting, payment, security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution or other lawful purposes. Backups may take time to expire. We will not promise immediate erasure from every backup unless the deployed system can provide it.

Connected services and content

Where supported, disconnect third-party integrations before or during the request. Data already shared with a third party may also need to be deleted by contacting that provider. Content that another user lawfully received or records an academy/coach is independently required to keep may be subject to separate obligations.

Contact and policies

For privacy questions or a deletion request, contact novacode.create@gmail.com. See also the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
